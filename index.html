02 The Game

      

        

          
Breach

          
Slip past cameras and crack HEINDE's systems through hacking minigames. Every change you make leaves a mark.

        

        

          
Tag

          
Art is illegal. Paint anyway. Tagged walls become your save points across the city.

        

        

          
Climb

          
A vertical city: the rich above, data centers below, and you somewhere in the remnants between.

        

        

          
Choose

          
No ending menu. The world bends around small decisions until it is unrecognisable.
